### RL-1 — Pinned-template release

- Date: 2026-10-21
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: SEND-001

Recorded operative text (unchanged from the pre-cleanup definition):

A sender prepares an envelope whose documents are bound by their SHA-256 fingerprints, with recipients, routing order and fields (`PREP-*`, `ROUTE-001`). Every send has a named authority, recorded on the envelope. An ad hoc send by a person requires that person's explicit confirmation that the document is eligible for electronic signature. A send from a template, a bulk batch (`BULK-001`), a web form (`FORM-001`), an API key or OAuth client with the send scope (`API-001`, `MOVE-007`), an integration (`INTEG-001`, `INTEG-002`, `INTEG-011`) or a schedule (`PREP-009`) goes out under the account's sending policy (`ADMIN-004`), which the owner or an admin set once: there is no per-envelope human approval, and the record names the policy, its version and the credential that used it. An internal approval rule (`ROUTE-004`) adds a person's approval wherever the policy demands one. After sending, the documents' bytes never change, and recipients, fields and settings change only through audited corrections (`DELIV-006`, `ROUTE-006`, `ROUTE-011`), which never alter a field already signed or a signature already given. Today's build is the narrow first-release case: one PDF, one to five ordered signers, ad hoc sends by a person only, and audited corrections by `DELIV-006`; that case is a subset of `ROUTE-001` (up to 100 recipients) and the policies above, never a limit on them. At scheduled dispatch and the last ROUTE-004 approval, Inkwell rechecks the confirming person's active account, current owner/admin authority or sender role plus ownership/edit share, and the unchanged sending definition. The definition fingerprint covers the PDF fingerprint, recipients, fields, free-form flag, copies, supplemental documents, messages, expiry, reminders, paper/reassignment options and template approval rule. The current envelope version, that authority and the `PREP-011` free-form setting version are fenced in the same atomic batch as invitations. A missing or stale confirmation, including a legacy deferred confirmation without this fingerprint, returns the envelope to a held draft, revokes its unused signing links and requires a current editor's fresh eligibility confirmation; restoring the previous person's role or share alone never releases it. An expired browser session alone does not cancel the person's recorded confirmation. Live recheck at every release (scope-review-65 A7): an immediate send, a scheduled send, the last `ROUTE-004` approval, each bulk row and each web-form release re-check, in the same atomic batch as the invitations, the current policy versions (`ADMIN-004`); that the credential is active, unexpired and not suspended; the principal's current status and role or profile; that the template live revision matches its confirmation snapshot; and the sending-definition fingerprint above. A failed re-check follows `ADMIN-004`'s hold rule. Built today for the ad hoc and template sources: the policy version and verdict are fenced in the batch that sends, releases a scheduled send or makes the last internal approval, and each source that adds a door (`BULK-001`, `FORM-001`, `API-001`, `MOVE-007`, `INTEG-*`) inherits the re-check before it ships.

Ct's 21 October template recheck ruling: preparation freezes the bytes and sending definition at the pinned template version.

Each eligibility confirmation records that template ID, pinned version and then-live revision, including its approval rule and sending-policy version.

Every immediate release, scheduled dispatch and last ROUTE-004 approval fences that live revision in the invitation batch.

Any intervening edit, new version, rule or sending-policy change, disablement or deletion holds the draft and revokes unused signing links; restoring an earlier state never releases it.

A current editor's fresh confirmation may release the unchanged older frozen definition and bytes, recording the new confirmer and time and re-snapshotting the live revision; it never adopts newer template content.

Preparing a newer definition requires a new draft version and confirmation.

Deletion keeps the frozen approval rule and never changes a sent envelope. Built evidence: `tests/send-policies.test.mjs` and `tests/journeys/template-release.mjs` exercise changed/deleted templates, held drafts, preserved frozen rules and fresh confirmation of unchanged bytes; API and public-form credential sends now ship. `tests/api-keys.test.mjs` — “API-001 and SEND-001: OpenAPI clients send only with explicit key scope and autonomous policy, with atomic creation, live deferred fences and fresh human recovery” proves create/send/poll/download/void, separate scope and agent refusal, API policy-off/person refusal with no envelope, source/principal/key/current-policy evidence, audit verification and idempotent creation, scheduled policy-off holds across off/on, revoked-key scheduled/final-approval holds, and explicit fresh personal confirmation of unchanged held drafts. `tests/journeys/internal-approval.mjs` proves policy-off and revoked-key holds and personal recovery on the built detail screen at 1440/390. `tests/web-forms.test.mjs` and `tests/journeys/forms.mjs` now prove its autonomous public-form and live form-credential points.

### RL-2 — Signer attachment custody

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: PREP-003

Recorded operative text (unchanged from the pre-cleanup definition):

Fields include signature, initials, date signed (filled automatically in the signer's US time zone), full name, email, company, title, text, number, checkbox, checkbox group with minimum and maximum, radio group, dropdown, attachment upload, stamp, and a note; each can be required or optional, read-only or editable, and validated by pattern (US phone, ZIP, SSN or EIN masked on display, email, currency in USD, date format). Each business-data field type added here inherits PREP-008’s prefill, reported source and read-only obligations before it ships.

Initials fields carry `SIGNX-002`'s obligation: before they ship, the signer adopts initials by typing, drawing or uploading them, the adopted initials go on every initials field, and the record and certificate keep the method.

Every new field type joins `SIGNX-003`'s accessibility scan and its keyboard-only and screen-reader journeys before it ships.

Round A adds initials, date signed, full name, email, company and title.

Date signed is generated from the server signing instant in the signer-selected US zone (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Arizona, Pacific, Alaska or Hawaii); sender prefill and caller-supplied dates cannot replace it.

Business-data fields accept PREP-008 prefill and reported sources with server-enforced read-only values; signatures, initials and generated dates are not business-data prefills.

Validation checks syntax, not identity or tax registration: ZIP is five digits with an optional four-digit suffix, US phone is ten digits with an optional country code 1 and conventional separators, SSN is nine digits or XXX-XX-XXXX, EIN is nine digits or XX-XXXXXXX, email needs a local part and dotted domain, and dates are real YYYY-MM-DD calendar dates.

SSN and EIN displays are always masked; other business fields may be masked by the sender. Masking changes field display on preparation/signing overlays and dashboard values, not the executed PDF or the existing authorized audit downloads. Initials are adopted separately from a signature and the same initials and mark apply to every filled initials field; a signed-in member's saved initials are offered on subsequent in-app signing, outside archives like their saved signature. Round B's choice/note slice adds radio groups, dropdowns, checkbox groups and single-line notes. Radio and dropdown values are one of the sender's exact option labels. A checkbox group's value is a JSON array of distinct declared labels, stored in declaration order and printed as a comma-separated list; minimum and maximum selection counts are inclusive whole numbers from zero to the option count. An answered group meets both bounds; a required group also needs at least one selection, while an optional group may be left blank. Choices declare 2–20 distinct single-line labels, each at most 100 characters and together at most 500 characters as JSON. Notes use the existing single-line text value and optional validation/masking. Each of these four types accepts sender/API prefill with a reported source and a server-enforced read-only setting, survives templates, copies and archive restore, and joins PREP-003's desktop/phone keyboard, accessibility-tree and light/dark scan journey. Image stamps also accept PNG or JPEG uploads of at most 512 KiB, using SIGNX-002’s actual image parser and size bound. Stamps accept sender/API prefill with a reported source and a read-only flag; the API value is a data URL containing the image bytes, kept with the field values through copies, templates and archives, protected and erased with the envelope. The executed PDF scales the image to fit its field without changing its proportions. Signing and preparation show an image preview unless masked; the dashboard shows an uploaded-image label, never its encoded bytes. A stamp is a supplied image, not a verified seal. The signing audit records each filled stamp's SHA-256, verified PNG/JPEG type, byte count and supplier: signer upload, or sender/API prefill with its reported source and read-only flag. An image equal to its prepared prefill retains that reported supplier, even when the signer submits the same bytes again. The certificate states `Stamp (<label>): <PNG|JPEG> SHA-256 <hex>, <supplier>; a supplied image, not a verified seal` (including source and read-only for prefills). Archive staging recomputes the image evidence and checks it against the signing audit; legacy signed stamps without separate evidence retain their original records and are checked against the signing event's existing values fingerprint. This is an implementation slice; PREP-003 remains partial. Attachments are unshipped: no attachment field or upload door is available. The attachment target is one parsed PDF, PNG or JPEG per field, at most 20 fields and 100,000,000 bytes per envelope, with 25,000,000 bytes per file in PREP-001 object storage. Shipping that target requires 20 MB JPEG/PDF success, 30 MB refusal, SHA-256 on read and restore, and legal hold, purge, erasure and restore-fence proof. Any future D1 slice must be labelled "attachment slice ≤2 MiB in D1 rows; 25 MB target awaits PREP-001 object storage; attachment acceptance not met" and name its real limit in refusals. The ct 20 October ruling also binds refusals (empty 400; too big 413; wrong type/leading bytes, corrupt/truncated files, encrypted PDFs or images over 100 MP 415), bounded-memory parsing without full decode, exact submitted bytes, uploader-only unsigned entries cleared on identity replacement or terminal action, immutability after signing, signed-byte audit/manifest/archive validation and retention fences. Attachments are never mailed or displayed except an uploader's PNG/JPEG preview before signing; downloads use the verified type and `<label>-<sha8>.<ext>` filename with "Uploaded by the signer · not scanned or opened by Inkwell". Files belong only to the uploader's place and ACCESS-001 record holders; other recipients receive fingerprint lines with type, byte count, SHA-256 and "not verified", with no filename. Wider recipient sharing waits for ROUTE-012. No malware scan, quarantine or provider is claimed. Attachments must join SIGNX-001, SIGNX-003 and PREP-005 before shipping; their full acceptance remains unasserted.

### RL-3 — Follow-up grant and private reply custody

- Date: 2026-10-22
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: AGENT-003

Recorded operative text (unchanged from the pre-cleanup definition):

*What the agent does:* watches each recipient's events (not opened, opened and left, bounced, out-of-office replies, repeated views), tells apart an unavailable signer, a bad address and a substantive objection, and follows up by allowed channels and times with a message suited to the cause; an objection goes to the sender (or Locket) instead of another reminder. *Data:* delivery and signing events, replies in the granted mailbox, the CRM owner. *Human control:* the sender sets channels, maximum contacts, quiet hours and the escalation owner; the agent never negotiates or changes the document. Exact semantics (ct, 22 Oct): a follow-up grant is an explicit, versioned, per-envelope authorization for Inkwell's built-in agent, given by whoever may change the envelope (ADMIN-001). It is never a credential, and no preparation token, API key or OAuth grant can create, use or read it. It sets channels (email; SMS with IDV-002), at most 1-10 contacts per recipient (default 3, counting DELIV-003 reminders sent under it), allowed days and hours in the account's US zone, and an escalation owner who can read the envelope. It authorizes only reading this envelope's delivery and signing events and its replies, sending fixed per-cause follow-ups with the current invitation link to current-turn recipients at most once per business day, and proposing actions to people. It never corrects, voids, extends, edits, answers or signs. Every action rechecks the grant, the bound member's authority, the envelope's open state and the recipient's turn and identity epoch in the same batch. Revocation takes effect for the next action and cancels scheduled follow-ups; the bound member's loss of authority suspends it permanently; a claimed send is never resent. Causes are deterministic ("model/provider: none"): DELIV-002 bounce = bad address; RFC 3834 auto-reply = unavailable until its explicit return date (else M business days); any other authenticated reply pauses follow-up to that recipient until a person resumes it and is routed to the sender and escalation owner; not-opened, opened-and-left and repeated-views get one follow-up each. A corrected address is only a cited proposal applied through DELIV-002. Replies arrive at a per-recipient, per-identity-epoch 256-bit Reply-To capability (purpose-separated HMAC of the random invitation identity; only its hash is stored; never archived; rotated on correction or reassignment; dead at close) through Resend's Svix-signed `email.received` webhook (5-minute window, replay refused) plus a fetch of the real message. They are accepted only with signature, capability and an exact current-recipient From; a mismatched or provider-failed sender is kept as "unverified sender" and never acted on; an unknown capability stores nothing. Text up to 500 kB and core headers are kept, larger bodies are labelled not kept, and attachments are listed by name, type, size and SHA-256 only. Reply text is read only by the sender, the escalation owner and those who may change the envelope, never by keys, tokens or other agents. It is absent from logs, audit and notifications, travels with export and archive, follows RECORD-005 holds, is erased by ERASE-001, and never appears on the certificate. Any model classification first meets PREP-006's enablement and disclosure terms. INTEG-004 and INTEG-005 (a connected Gmail or Outlook mailbox as a reply source), IDV-002 (SMS follow-up), INTEG-008 (objections routed to Locket), INTEG-001..003 (CRM owner as default escalation owner), ADMIN-004 and TMPL-001 (follow-up defaults), ADMIN-005 (grant events), ADMIN-007 (other inbound providers) and AGENT-009 (the account's inbound address) each carry these rules in their own text and acceptance before they ship.

### RL-7 — Embedded signing and sending

- Date: 2026-10-26
- Ruler: ct
- Governs: API-003

Recorded operative text (unchanged from the pre-cleanup definition):

An application embeds signing through a recipient view URL or web component in an iframe, receives postMessage events for signed, declined and closed, and embeds preparing and sending in its own pages, restricted to allowed origins.

**CT policy ruling, 26 October 2026: APPROVED WITH RIDERS.** This approves product defaults only. Implementation, provider operation, legal suitability and release acceptance require separate evidence. At the reviewed pin the clause remained partial; subsequent implementation evidence is recorded in Status.

**Origin authority and consent.** Embedding starts denied. Only a signed-in owner or admin may grant, change or revoke an embedding origin and explicitly authorize a launch. Granting or widening an origin and authorizing preparation require a native sign-in no older than 15 minutes. Consent records the actor, time, consent-text version, exact parent origin, purpose, applicable grant version and launch expiry. Launch consent identifies the envelope and recipient for signing, or the single draft allocation for preparation. It discloses that the parent receives only the permitted status events. It authorizes neither signing on another person’s behalf nor automatic sending, additional envelopes, document disclosure to the parent or wider API authority. Recipient disclosure and consent to sign remain separate.

**Exact origins.** Production grants accept HTTPS origins only. Matching uses the parsed, canonical origin tuple: scheme, canonical host and effective port. Host case and an explicitly written default HTTPS port do not create distinct origins. Subdomains, other ports and other schemes do not match. Wildcards, opaque or null origins, credentials, paths other than the root, queries and fragments are refused. An origin change revokes the old grant version and its outstanding launches and embedded sessions; the replacement requires explicit consent. Removal and later re-addition never revive old authority.

**Launch and session custody.** A launch credential expires 15 minutes after issuance and is redeemable once, atomically, for one embedded session. Replaying it creates no session, draft or invitation. A failed response after successful redemption does not make the credential reusable; recovery requires a fresh authorized launch. The session expires no later than the launch’s original expiry or an earlier underlying authority expiry. It is bound to the installation, exact parent origin, purpose and grant version, and to the envelope and recipient identity epoch for signing or the authorizing member and one allocated envelope for preparation.
